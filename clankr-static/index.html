02Mechanism

Holder rewards and protocol burns.

01

Holder Rewards

CLANKER pays 85% of trading fees to holders from every paired liquidity pool. Rewards are auto-distributed in ROBOSTRATEGY — a robotics RWA token — so holders gain direct exposure to physical-AI equity just by holding CLANKER.

02

Auto Buyback & Burn

StonkFun's protocol automatically buys and burns CLANKER from the open market every 5 minutes using the remaining fee share. Supply only goes down — every burn is permanent and verifiable onchain.

Tokenized robotics equities track prices during and outside market hours. Prices can move while the underlying market is closed, and liquidity is thinner then. CLANKER is a memecoin with no intrinsic value and no expectation of financial return. Not affiliated with Robostrategy.
